About Me

I am currently a postdoctoral fellow in the School of Mathematical Sciences at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, working with Professor Jinwei Yang. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Nanjing University in 2026 under the supervision of Professor Gongxiang Liu, and my B.S. in Mathematics from Nanjing University in 2021. [Ph.D. Thesis]

Research Interests

My research primarily focuses on algebraic structures, including:

  • Hopf and Coquasi-Hopf Algebras
  • Tensor Categories
  • Nichols Algebras
  • Vertex Operator Algebras

In particular, I am interested in reflection theory and classification problems for Nichols algebras and pointed coquasi-Hopf algebras.

Preprints

Taken together, these three preprints develop a reflection theory for Nichols algebras over coquasi-Hopf algebras with bijective antipode. For a tuple of finite-dimensional simple Yetter–Drinfeld modules admitting all reflections, the theory associates a Cartan graph and its Weyl groupoid and provides criteria for the finite-dimensionality of the corresponding Nichols algebra.

  • On the Cartan Graphs of Nichols Algebras over Coquasi-Hopf Algebras
    B. Li. arXiv:2607.05065. [PDF]
  • Reflection Theory of Nichols Algebras over Coquasi-Hopf Algebras with Bijective Antipode
    B. Li, G. Liu. arXiv:2602.07946. [PDF]
  • Reflection of Nichols Algebras over Coquasi-Hopf Algebras
    B. Li, G. Liu. arXiv:2512.04560. [PDF]
